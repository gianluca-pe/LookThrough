How updates and corrections work

Each view uses the latest saved set on or before its date. Rates share one reference date; they are indicative consolidation rates, not bank dealing prices. Dates before your first reference set keep using your earlier individual rates.

We fetch only the latest published table. Identical updates do nothing. A changed rate for an already saved date requires explicit replacement; the previous edition stays in your backup. Older dates are not polled. If a download fails or its format changes, the message explains why and manual entry remains available.
