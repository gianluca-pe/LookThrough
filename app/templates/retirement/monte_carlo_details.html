Model: {{ result.model_version }} · Seed: {{ result.seed }} · {{ result.path_count }} paths per allocation. Decimal precision 28, round-half-even; integer MT19937 draws with a Decimal polar-normal transform. Annual log-return centre = log(1 + entered compound growth). Percentiles linearly interpolate sorted balances at (path count − 1) × percentile.

Opening obligations are settled before lifestyle spending. Dated income then funds Core and Flexible, with any surplus available to rebalance. Spending occurs before returns. Year-end net savings start earning the following year and carry no inherited account locks. Taxes and trading costs are not deducted.

Locked assets earn model returns but cannot be withdrawn or rebalanced. Account access and FD maturity must both permit access at an annual boundary. Maturity unlocks existing capital; it creates no additional principal. FDs use the fixed Liquidity return rather than individual contractual interest. Available funds fill role shortfalls after accounting for locked exposures. The deterministic retirement projection continues to use its own bucket funding order.

These results are repeatable while inputs and model version match. They are not a saved historical run. Source changes require a fresh comparison; keep a local printed copy for your records.
