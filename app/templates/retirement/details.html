The solver finds the largest annual Flexible allowance, to the cent, that pays Core and Flexible in every retirement year and meets the end goal. Core remains your input. If Core alone fails, Flexible is zero; Core is never silently reduced.

The spending chart shows nominal amounts paid in each year, including inflation. Its dashed reference line shows those same actual payments in portfolio-date purchasing power, including any payment shortfall. The ending-capital chart also uses purchasing power on the portfolio date; its selected-year readout shows the nominal balance alongside it. The final-age goal is shown in both bases. Core, Flexible and the final goal use one inflation rate. Spending is paid at the start of each retirement year, before returns; end balances are deflated from the end-of-year boundary to the portfolio date. The year starting one age before the final age is the last spending year.

Working years have no portfolio-funded living costs. Net savings are added at year-end, earn returns the following year and stop when retirement spending begins. Savings use the starting non-Project portfolio role and bucket mix, held fixed for new contributions. They are modelled as separate accessible contributions, independently of the original accounts and their existing locks. Existing holdings are not rebalanced.

Planned income is available only at retirement-year boundaries within its entered dates. Its own entered escalation is preserved. Income pays opening obligations, then Core, then Flexible; surplus remains cash. Withdrawals use Cash → Now → Bridge → Growth. Existing account access and FD maturity independently limit availability; mixed holdings are sold proportionally.

Returns are owner-entered nominal total returns applied once yearly to remaining capital. No additional taxes, fees, transactions or random return sequences are invented. The simulation never posts to the ledger. Recalculate from actual balances at the next review; more or less capital can change the affordable allowance in either direction.
