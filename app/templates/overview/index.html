Spending, access and bucket assumptions

Allocation percentages use positive starting cash plus the current Now, Bridge and Growth values included in this reserve model, after account access and excluding Projects. Term deposits remain in that starting-capital total even when maturity makes them unavailable for an early bucket. An existing cash deficit increases Now's requirement instead of reducing this denominator. Incomplete sources or zero capital withhold percentages.

Accessible cash is assigned once: Now first, then Bridge, up to each requirement. Any excess stays in unused cash. After cash, unused earlier buckets are applied before the period's own bucket and then later buckets. No holding is reclassified.

Values reflect your portfolio share and present account access. Fixed deposits qualify only in the period containing their maturity or a later period. This compares whole periods; it does not test each annual payment date within them or assume future account access.

The Core budget at the selected date is held constant for all ten years; Flexible spending and future income are excluded. Without an adopted plan, the unsplit annual budget is used and labelled explicitly. Real asset values are also held constant before spending; inflation adds nothing to either side of this reserve check. Your saved inflation and return assumptions continue to apply in the Retirement planner. Projects stay excluded{% if funding.projects_included_amount %} ({{ money({"amount": funding.projects_included_amount, "currency": funding.reporting_currency}) }} included value){% endif %}.

Liquidity and Income are asset roles; Now and Bridge are funding buckets. A bucket can contain Equity-exposed holdings. The source detail reports that exposure without splitting mixed holdings into separate investments.
