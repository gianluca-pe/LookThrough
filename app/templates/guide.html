Where things belong

Institution
The bank, broker, insurer, or provider.
Account
The real custody or statement container at that institution. Do not invent an umbrella account merely to group fixed deposits.
Holding
An investment, opaque wrapper, or fixed deposit held inside an account.
Activity
A Buy, Sell, dividend, or other recorded change. Use it for quantity-tracked investments—not to onboard an existing fixed deposit.
Current value
Refresh prices, statement values, cash confirmations, and FX from Update values.

Fixed deposits

  1. Choose the real account that holds or funds the deposit.
  2. Add the fixed deposit with its current principal and date.
  3. Continue to its bank terms: start, maturity, expected proceeds, rate, and intended maturity action.
  4. At maturity, record the actual disposition to cash or rollover.

Adding an already-open deposit does not post a cash movement. Confirmed account cash should reflect the bank statement and exclude principal already placed in the deposit.
